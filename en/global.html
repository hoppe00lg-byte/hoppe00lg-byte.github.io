Three global business tools — trade email, trip settlement and company checks on one page

Three things a trader actually uses every day, on one page. Pick the recipient country and a trade email written in that language and to its etiquette comes out; pick a destination and per diem, hotel ceilings, duty-free limits, voltage and units are laid out together; and you can check the registration, financials and shipment history of companies in the US, China, Japan, the UK, India and Vietnam on their official sites. No sign-up, everything runs in your browser, and nothing you type leaves it.
