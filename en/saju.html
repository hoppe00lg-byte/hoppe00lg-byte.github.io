Calculation criteria

If you were born before the first day of spring, the year is considered to have not changed and is calculated according to the previous year's zodiac sign. This is why people born in January are in the previous year’s Chinese Zodiac sign.

The 12 verses of Sohan, Ipchun, Gyeongchip, Cheongmyeong, Ipha, Mangjong, Soseo, Ipchu, Baekro, Hanro, Ipdong, and Daeseol are the boundaries of the month. Monthly periods are marked with five horoscopes, which are determined according to the celestial phase of the year.
