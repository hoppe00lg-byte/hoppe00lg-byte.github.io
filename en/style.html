Three steps is all it takes

HOW TO
STEP 01

Just height, weight and occasion

Gender, height and weight produce BMI and a body type. If the calculation feels off, tap a body-type chip and pick your own. There are four occasions: work, date, weekend and travel.

STEP 02

The photo is optional

A close face shot gives skin tone; a full-body shot gives the shoulder-to-hip ratio. The image is processed only in an on-device canvas, never sent, and disappears on reload.

STEP 03

Copy keywords, save the lookbook

Paste each set's shopping keyword into a shop search box and similar items come up. Save the three sets as a 1080×1350 lookbook PNG to send to a friend or to pack for a trip.

FAQ

8 answers
Does my photo get uploaded?
No. The browser reads the file into memory, draws it on a canvas to compute pixel values, then discards it. No image goes over the network — you can confirm this in your browser's network tab.
How accurate is the personal-colour reading?
What the photo gives is a reference value: colour temperature of the light, makeup, background and camera white balance all move it. The tool splits warm, cool and neutral by the brightness and yellow-to-red balance of skin pixels, then picks a seasonal palette by brightness. For an exact reading, a professional in-person consultation is better.
The body type differs from what I know
BMI only sees height and weight, so it cannot reflect the shoulder-to-hip ratio. A photo adds that ratio, but arm position and clothing shift it. Pick a body-type chip and that choice takes priority.
How often do the trends change?
The trend data behind the suggestions is refreshed weekly. It currently carries the 2026 autumn/winter runways — soft tailoring, wide pants, midnight bloom, shearling, high-neck double coats, vintage knits — plus the 2027 early-spring direction. The update date is shown under the results.
How do I use the shopping keywords?
Each one is a search phrase built from a colour and an item name — searching "butter white turtleneck knit" brings up items in a similar colour and fit. The copy button puts it on your clipboard.
What is the lookbook image for?
It is a 1080×1350 PNG with all three sets on one card — handy for a packing list, a shopping note or sending to a friend. On mobile the share sheet sends it directly.
Does it work for men or unisex styles?
Yes. Choose men or unisex and it only mixes items that fit — suits, shirts, knits and wide pants lead the way.
What if I don't want to give height and weight?
Pick a body-type chip and leave height and weight as they are — you still get outfits. Only the BMI figure may not match you. Nothing you type is sent or stored.

Privacy & copyright

Photo processing — An uploaded photo is read only in browser memory and a canvas; once the analysis ends nothing is kept but the on-screen preview. Nothing is sent or stored, so the operator cannot see it either. If someone else appears in the photo, get their consent first.

Inputs — Height, weight, body type and occasion are not stored in cookies or on a server. Reloading resets them.

What the recommendation is — The tool assembles its text from trend data summarised from public runway and fashion-press reporting, plus the values you enter. It does not recommend or advertise any brand or product, and has no affiliate relationship with any shop.

How the photo is used — The result image (lookbook PNG) does not include your photo. It is drawn from colours and item names only.

This tool is a reference for shopping and outfit decisions. Body-type and skin-tone readings are estimates from your photo and inputs, not medical or cosmetic diagnoses. The trend data's date is shown with the results.
