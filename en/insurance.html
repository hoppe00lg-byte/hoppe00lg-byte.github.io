01Keep your plan vs 4th vs 5th generation

INDEMNITY SWITCH

Later generations look cheaper on premium alone, but in a year with hospital bills you also pay deductibles and a surcharge on the non-covered premium. The three contracts are lined up by actual annual cost — premium plus deductible — and the gap is calculated.

The 5th-generation plan arrived on 6 May 2026 — new 4th-generation sign-ups and switches have ended

The 4th generation transition was subject to contracts concluded until May 5, 2026. The new product that can be switched to now is the 5th generation, and the 4th generation item in this calculator is a reference value to review the profit or loss when you have already subscribed to the 4th generation or changed from the 1st to 3rd generations to the 4th generation. You can decide between maintenance and 5th generation.

  • Premium — the 5th generation is about 30% cheaper than the 4th and at least 50% cheaper than the 1st and 2nd. Covering only standard care and severe non-covered care can halve it.
  • Non-covered care — severe cases (those under the special calculation scheme) have a 30% deductible and a 50 million won annual limit; mild cases have a 50% deductible and a 10 million won limit.
  • Excluded — manual therapy, extracorporeal shockwave, non-covered injections and unlisted new medical technology are not covered by the mild non-covered rider.
  • Standard care — admission stays at 20%; outpatient care is linked to the national health insurance co-payment and can exceed 20%.

Source: Announcement of the launch of 5th generation indemnity health insurance by the Financial Services Commission and Financial Supervisory Service (2026-05-05), comparison of 4th and 5th generation product structures by the Non-life Insurance Association.

What is included and what is not
  • Included — 12 months of monthly premium, benefit/non-benefit co-payment rate, discount (-5%)/surcharge (+100/200/300%) for 4th generation non-benefit special contract and 5th generation non-heavy special contract, and grade according to non-benefit insurance payment amount.
  • 5th generation non-covered medical expenses — Non-covered medical expenses are divided into severe, non-covered, and non-severe and calculated as 30% for severe, 50% for non-severe, and 100% for non-severe, and the annual limit of 10 million won applies to non-severe insurance premiums. Severe cases are limited to 50 million won per year.
  • What is not included — Minimum copayment per outpatient visit (10,000 won for 1st to 3rd generation hospital/clinic, 20,000 won for advanced comprehensive, 30,000 won for non-insurance for 4th generation, 50,000 won for non-intensive care for 5th generation). If outpatient visits are frequent and medical expenses are small, the actual out-of-pocket burden will be greater than calculated.
  • If the out-of-pocket cost for hospitalization for the 4th and 5th generations exceeds 2 million won per year, the excess is compensated, and for the 5th generation, there is a cap of 5 million won per year for out-of-pocket expenses when hospitalized at a tertiary general or general hospital. These upper limits are not taken into account.
  • The 2-year accident-free 10% discount for the 5th generation and the overlapping application of the accident-free discount were not included.
  • Contract conversion discount — When toggled on, 50% of the 5th generation monthly premium is applied only to the 1st to 3rd years, and from the 4th year onwards, the premium is calculated before the discount. Discounts are only applied to insurance premiums and not to deductibles.
  • With the conversion discount turned on, the overlapping method of non-coverage premium levels and discounts cannot be reflected according to the insurance company's terms (the premium rate is applied to the insurance premium as is). Changes in insurance premiums due to renewal during the discount period are not included.
  • The premium level is based on the insurance payment date, not the date of treatment, and is determined by one year of performance starting from the end of three months before the contract date. If you collect your claims, you may be able to increase your rating.
  • Salary and non-salaried insurance premiums are adjusted again according to the loss ratio at renewal, so the amount may vary after 2 to 3 years.

      How the surcharge is determined
      • Compare the material accident surcharge standard (500,000, 1 million, 1.5 million, 2 million won) selected at the time of subscription with the material accident insurance amount paid by the insurance company. This amount is not the amount you pay, but is the standard for determining whether your grade will change.
      • An accident below the standard amount is worth 0.5 accident points per incident, if it exceeds the standard and your car insurance premium is less than 100 million won, you get 1 point, and if it exceeds 100 million won, you get 2 points. An accident score of 1 point is the basis for a 1-level change in discount surcharge level.
      • Even for accidents below the standard amount, if there are two accidents within three years, an additional premium may be applied, and even if the grade remains the same, the renewal insurance premium may rise due to the characteristic rate according to the number of accidents.
      • If the damage is total or the amount to be compensated is more than the subscription amount, the deductible is not deducted (the contract for the car is terminated).
      • The grading process may be different for accidents where fault has not been determined or no-fault accidents, so check with the insurance company before filing.
