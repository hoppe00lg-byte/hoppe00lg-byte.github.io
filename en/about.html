ABOUT THE DESK

Context behind the numbers
Reading editing room

INSIGHT BRIEFis an independent research webzine that tracks the economy, finance, trade, raw materials, and AI and tech through data and interviews. We prioritize confirmed context over quick news, and verifiable records over conclusions.

What do you see?

We start from the industrial field. We look at where raw materials come from, at what stage prices are attached, and how regulations are translated between paper and actual processes. Economic indicators are used as a starting point for questions, not as results.

The article is broadly divided into four parts. Investigationdeals with facts confirmed through records and interviews. Economy & Financerepresents interest rates, prices, and household numbers, Trade & Commoditiesshows the flow of raw materials and supply chain, AI & Techcovers how technology is changing the way we work.

Editorial Principles

  1. distinguish between confirmed and assumed. Facts, statements by officials, and interpretation by the editorial office are displayed separately in the sentence.
  2. I leave the source. At the end of the article, write down the data you confirmed and the scope of the interview. For anonymous reporters, the editorial office records the reason for their anonymity.
  3. This provides an opportunity for rebuttal. The parties who have been the subject of criticism are requested to provide sufficient time before publication.
  4. Disclosure of conflict of interest. The editorial office's interests in relation to reporting or advertising are disclosed in the article or editorial office notes.
  5. Corrections are not hidden. If an error is confirmed, the date and content of the correction will be left in the article, and if necessary, a separate correction article will be published.
This webzine is not a registered Internet newspaper in Korea. It does not meet registration requirements, such as having more than 5 full-time reporting and editing staff, and does not use official media names such as ‘OO Ilbo’ or ‘OO News.’ As an independent research media, we publish articles and records.

Fact checking and corrections

All reports are cross-checked at least once before publication. Statistics check the reference point and definition of the original data, and citations are compared with the original text. Any unconfirmed information should be omitted from the article or written as ‘unconfirmed’.

Separation of advertising, sponsorship and editing

We may receive banner advertisements and sponsored content for operation. Advertising contracts do not influence the content of our coverage, and we do not accept advertisements conditioned on a specific tone of voice. Sponsored content is included in the title and at the beginning of the text. ‘Advertisement’ or ‘Sponsorship’and is distinguished by a different font and label from regular articles.

Personal information and informant protection

The report form does not store any content on the server. The contact information you sent will only be used to respond for reporting purposes and will not be disclosed without the informant's consent. Personal information learned during the reporting process will be destroyed once the purpose has been achieved.

However, if there is a legitimate request from an investigative agency, we may cooperate in accordance with relevant laws and regulations. If you wish to report anonymously, please leave your name and contact information blank and send it to a separate anonymous email.
