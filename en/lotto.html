How does this tool create numbers?

The number is In the browser is created. When you press the Create button, 6 numbers from 1 to 45 will be drawn without duplicates, and only the weight to be drawn for each number will vary depending on the method selected. In random mode, all numbers have the same probability, and in frequency/non-appearance mode, the number calculated from the most recent 100 records is used as the weight. Fixed numbers and excluded numbers are applied together in any method.

The winning numbers are based on the results for each round released by the Donghaeng Lottery. When you enter the page, the latest round is checked first, and if the new round has not yet been reflected, the last verified round and drawing date are displayed on the card. Recent Frequency Weighting
The more numbers that have appeared in the last 100 rounds, the higher the chance of being selected. The method is to follow the records.
Non-appearance number weighting
The more times there are since the last one, the higher the chance. This is the commonly referred to ‘number that hasn’t been out for a long time.’
Reflects my conditions
The selected fixed numbers must be included in the result, and excluded numbers do not appear. The remaining positions are filled randomly.
Comparison of all-time 1st to 5th places
Match the 6 numbers you created with the winning numbers from the previous round, one by one, and count how many times you would have ranked 1st to 5th if you had been that number. It is only a comparison with past records and has nothing to do with future results.
