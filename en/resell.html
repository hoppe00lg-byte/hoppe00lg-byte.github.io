How to read the numbers

4 points
01

Settlement and net profit differ

The settlement is what the platform pays into your account after taking its fee. Net profit is what is left once the purchase price, shipping, duty and VAT and packing come off that. A large settlement can still leave a negative net.

02

Return is measured against cost

Return on cost (ROI) here is net profit ÷ total cost. It is not the same number as margin on the sale price, so keep them apart. Put in ₩1m and keep ₩200,000 and the ROI is 20%, while against a ₩1.2m sale price it is 16.7%.

03

Break-even purchase price

Above this price you cannot make money even after selling. Use it as your line when negotiating or bidding. Where duty applies the tax climbs with the price too, so break-even jumps sharply around the exemption limit.

04

Duty and VAT sit on the customs value

Duty is charged on the goods value plus international freight (the customs value), and 10% VAT is then charged on the customs value plus the duty. An 8% duty works out to an 18.8% effective burden, and 13% to 24.3%.

FAQ

8 answers
When does import duty apply in the US?
A shipment valued at US$800 or less enters duty-free under Section 321 (the de minimis rule), which covers most single-item purchases. Above US$800 the shipment needs a formal or informal entry and duty follows the HTS classification of the goods. There is no federal VAT; state and local sales tax is collected by the marketplace from the buyer at checkout, not by customs.
I am buying to resell — can I still use the US$800 exemption?
Section 321 is written for personal-use shipments. Goods imported for resale are business imports: they can need a formal entry, an importer number and duty from the first dollar. Many small resellers ship personal-use quantities, but the classification is decided by CBP, not by the calculator. Ask a customs broker before scaling a route that depends on the exemption.
Why do sneakers show 8% on eBay but 13.6% elsewhere?
In eBay’s two Athletic Shoes categories the final value fee is 8% of the total sale once it reaches US$150, with no per-order fee; below US$150 it is 13.6% plus $0.40. Other categories pay 13.6% up to US$7,500 per item plus the per-order fee. The preset labelled “sneakers $150+” is that 8% line.
How is the StockX fee calculated?
StockX Verified Marketplace charges a transaction fee by seller level (9% at Level 1 down to 7% at Level 5) plus a 3% payment processing fee, with a US$5 minimum, and the seller pays shipping (US$5 for standard sales). The preset uses Level 1: 12% plus the minimum fee.
What about Poshmark and Mercari?
Poshmark takes a flat 20% of the sale price. Mercari US charges 10% and pays the seller’s shipping label out of the sale. Both are in the preset list, so you can compare the same sale price across six marketplaces.
Why is there no VAT line in my result?
The United States has no federal VAT. Sales tax is a state and local tax collected from the buyer by the marketplace at checkout, so it never enters the seller’s settlement. That is why the VAT line stays at zero on this page — the duty line is the only import charge to watch.
What is the difference between return on cost (ROI) and margin?
Return on cost measures what you earned against the money you put in: net profit ÷ total cost. Margin is what is left against the sale price: net profit ÷ sale price. The sale price is larger than the cost, so margin is always the smaller number. The line under the calculator shows both.
Do I need a business to resell?
Selling regularly for profit is a business activity for the IRS, and most states expect a sales tax permit and a resale certificate to buy inventory without paying sales tax. This calculator shows one deal’s cash flow only; business accounting, quarterly estimates and deductions belong with an accountant.

Scope — A reference calculator that adds and subtracts settlement and net profit from the purchase price, exchange rate, shipping and fees you enter. Actual duty depends on the HTS classification, country of origin, entry type and CBP’s decision; there is no federal VAT at import, and state and local sales tax is collected by the marketplace from the buyer. Individual consumption tax, inspection and quarantine costs, returns and exchanges are not included.
