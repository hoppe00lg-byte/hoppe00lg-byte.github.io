Use these 4 tools to save time

Uploading a product is divided into taking a photo, creating a representative image, creating a detailed page, refining the title and tags, and creating a promotional link. Normally, these five steps involve going back and forth between different editors and sites, but here: to end within one page Tied. All calculations and image creation are done within the browser, and uploaded photos and entered text are not stored anywhere.

① Thumbnails have half the ‘ratio’ and ‘text size’

The representative image of Smart Store and Coupang is square. As it appears small in the list, the letters are copied from the 1000×1000 original. 6-8% of total width size, it will also be read in the mobile list. Two or fewer badges are recommended. After three, the badge appears before the product, making it difficult to recognize which product it is.

  • 1000×1000 — Smart Store·Coupang representative image, Instagram feed
  • 800×800 — Old seller center form and some open market specifications
  • 1200×628 — Blog·KakaoTalk·Facebook sharing card (1.91:1)

② The width of 860px is acceptable for the detail page.

The Naver Smart Store detail page and blog text cannot fit on one screen on mobile devices. 860pxis a standard that has been used for a long time as a width that is clear on PC and does not cut left or right on mobile. Image blocks are connected by maintaining the original ratio, and text blocks are placed with 60px left and right margins for easy reading. In card news mode, one block becomes a 1080×1080 card, so it is recommended to include only one message per block.

③ SEO is more about ‘place of keywords’ rather than ‘number of characters’

Titles within 25 to 40 characters are not truncated in search results. Target keyword is title Within the first 20 characters, once in the first paragraph of the body, and once in the subheading. Body keyword density is 1~2%is natural, and if it exceeds 3%, it is read as repetition. Instead of increasing the same words, replace them with related words (e.g. 'free shipping' → 'shipping fee of 0 won', 'same-day shipping').

  • In mobile search results, titles are truncated at approximately 2 lines and descriptions at approximately 80 characters.
  • Hashtags must match the content actually covered in the text to help with exposure.
  • The number of bytes is used to check storage and transmission limits (e.g., 100 bytes for product name).

④ QR·UTM is a device that leaves behind ‘where it came from’

QR can not only contain links. If you include contact information (vCard), it will be saved to your contacts with a single scan, and if you include Wi-Fi information, store visitors can access it without having to ask for a password. When inserting into print Margin 4 modules, large contrast difference between foreground and background, a size of 1024px or larger is recommended. UTM is utm_source(where) · utm_medium(some way) · utm_campaign(for any campaign) Three values are the default, and if these values are different for the same link, they are counted separately in the analysis tool.
