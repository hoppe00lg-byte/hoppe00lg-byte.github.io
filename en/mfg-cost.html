Calculation basis How it is calculated
  • Material cost = part weight × (1 + scrap rate) × resin price + the same weight × masterbatch % × additive price
  • Moulding cost = (cycle time ÷ 3,600) × (machine rate + labour rate) ÷ (cavities × (1 − defect rate))
  • Mould share = mould cost ÷ amortisation volume. With ‘all on this order’ it is cost ÷ order quantity. If the quantity exceeds the mould life, the cost grows by the number of mould sets needed.
  • Setup share = initial setup cost ÷ quantity
  • Post-processing and packaging are entered per good part; freight is converted to a per-piece figure from either the per-piece or the lump-sum value.
  • Outsourcing converts a per-piece, per-shot or per-hour quote to a per-piece price and, with free issue, adds the material cost. The admin fee is a percentage of the processing price.
  • Break-even quantity (BEP) = (in-house fixed − outsourced fixed) ÷ (outsourced variable − in-house variable). Fixed costs are the mould, setup and lump-sum freight; variable costs are material, processing, post-processing, packaging and per-piece freight. It assumes the quantity stays within the mould life.
  • Tax (VAT), financing, warehousing, certification and inspection costs are not included, nor is a payback period for equipment. Add them separately before deciding.

Calculation basis and privacy

Scope — a reference calculator that adds up unit cost and total from the quantity, prices and conditions you enter. An actual quote or closing also reflects tax, overhead, exchange-rate movement, utilisation and inspection or certification costs. This calculator does not include VAT, financing or warehousing.

Privacy — the item name, quantity, prices and exchange rate you enter are used only inside your browser and are not sent to a server. Values stay in this browser (localStorage) and disappear when you press ‘Clear inputs’ or clear browser data.

Resin prices — preset prices are public quotes converted to KRW/kg and exclude freight, duty, customs clearance and other charges. Confirm with a supplier quote before contracting.

This calculator is a reference tool for reviewing cost; it does not replace legal or commercial judgement about a contract or a quote. The figures shown are the result of the conditions entered and may differ from the actual quote or closing.
