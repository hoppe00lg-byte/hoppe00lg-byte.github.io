Reading the total cost

4 points
01

O/F is only half the story

A low O/F can still lose on total once BAF/CAF and destination D/THC, D/O and document fees are added. When comparing quotes, include the money that leaves at the destination.

02

Destination charges mix currencies

D/THC often arrives in USD while inland haulage and customs fees are quoted locally. Pick the currency for each line and this calculator converts everything at one rate.

03

Look again per tonne

If each container carries a different weight, comparing totals alone misleads. The cost per tonne and per kg shows which carrier is really cheaper.

04

Check the quote's validity

Spot rates move within days. Check the quote's validity, routing and space, then confirm the final amount with the carrier or forwarder.

Frequently asked questions

5 questions
Which costs should I enter?
Copy the lines from the carrier's quote: ocean freight, BAF and CAF, destination D/THC, D/O and document fees, inland haulage and the customs fee. VAT and duty are separate and are not included here.
Can I compare LCL shipments?
Yes. Choose LCL as the container type and compare totals plus cost per tonne and per kg. LCL rates are often per CBM, so check whether the quote is based on volume or weight.
Where does the exchange rate come from?
The default comes from the dollar rate on this site's commodities and FX board. It is the board's latest value, so it can differ from your bank's settlement rate or the carrier's rate. Edit the field and the calculation uses your number.
Can I get quotes without a carrier account?
Schedules and tariff tables are mostly public; instant (spot) quoting may require a login. With Korean and Taiwanese carriers, the practical route is to check the schedule on the official site and then ask a local agent or forwarder for the rate.
The result differs from the invoice — why?
This tool only totals the figures you enter. Surcharge periods, space availability, the rate date and terminal-specific charges all change the real invoice. Confirm the final amount with the carrier's or forwarder's quote and invoice.

Calculation basis and privacy

Scope — This is a calculator that converts and adds the freight, surcharges and charges you enter. Duty, VAT, insurance and inspection fees are not included and the total can differ from the carrier's invoice. Check the quote's validity and conditions with the carrier or forwarder.

Privacy — Carrier names, amounts and rates are calculated only in your browser and are not stored on any server. Reloading clears them, so print or save the table as a PDF if you need it.

Carrier links — Carrier names and trademarks belong to their owners; the links simply point to official websites for convenience. This page does not partner with carriers or broker freight.

This calculator is a reference for comparing import logistics costs. It is not a substitute for commercial or legal judgment on freight, schedules or customs, and the sample figures are unrelated to real market rates.
