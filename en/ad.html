Insight Briefs is an independent research webzine that tracks where raw materials come from, where prices are attached, and how regulations are translated between paper and field. We accept requests for advertising space, sponsor positions, and import/sourcing work on this one page.

What kind of medium is it?

As of November 23, 2026

Economic and raw material research using numbers and records

It is published in four sections: economy/finance, trade/raw materials, AI/tech, and investigative reporting. All articles are based on publicly available notices, laws, statistics, and press releases, and the sources and limitations are listed in the fact check table at the end of the article. I do not use numbers filled with estimates.

  • Full article published, no membership registration, no paid payments
  • Free raw material market information (oil price, exchange rate, Chinese futures, sea freight, petrochemical unit)
  • Three free tools: BaZi chart, Powerball & Mega Millions generator, US take-home pay calculator
  • RSS, site map, Naver/Google search exposure, social sharing card response

The number of visitors and subscribers varies depending on the time, so the numbers are not fixed on this page. If you inquire, we will summarize the actual figures for the last 7 days and 30 days.

This kind of reader reads

Practitioners who buy and sell raw materials, packaging materials, and parts, people in charge of imports and distribution, reporters and researchers who track supply chains and regulations, and individual investors who want to see the market in numbers.

Since one article is searched for a long time, the banner is used more than ‘one big time’. Visible continuously for a month is correct. For new products, exhibitions, and B2B services, it is better to use a leaderboard at the bottom of the article and a newsletter sponsor.
