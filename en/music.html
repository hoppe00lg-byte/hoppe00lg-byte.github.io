How to paste into Suno & Flow Music

3 steps
STEP 01

Style field: English tags only

Suno's Style of Music field is length-limited, so short tags work better. Paste the Suno style line above (under 200 characters) as it is — genre, BPM, mood and vocal are already in one line.

STEP 02

Lyrics field: keep the section tags

In Custom Mode, paste the section tags such as [Verse 1] and [Chorus] along with the lines — without them the song tends to fall apart. Bracketed tags are not sung.

STEP 03

Fix one line, not the whole song

Regenerating the same lyrics changes the melody. To change one section only, copy the ChatGPT / Gemini prompt and ask the model to “rewrite verse 2”.

Frequently asked questions

9 questions
Do I own the lyrics this tool makes?
The lyrics are a writing aid assembled from fixed sentence frames and word banks — editing a line or two yourself will always read better than using them as they are. Copyright and revenue sharing for AI-generated songs are set by each service's terms (Suno, Flow Music and others). Before any commercial use, check the paid-plan terms of that platform.
How many tags should go into the Suno style field?
Short and specific works best — five to eight tags in the order genre, BPM, mood, vocal, instruments. This tool builds the under-200-character line in that order and also shows an extended version for tools that allow longer prompts, such as Flow Music.
How do I keep Korean lyrics from slurring?
Choose Korean as the lyrics language and check that the style line contains korean lyrics. When Korean sits under English tags, avoid long words and keep each line within roughly 12 to 16 characters — it is easier to make out.
Can I write lyrics in Japanese or English?
Yes. Choose Japanese or English and the lyrics come out in that language's sentence frames. Style tags stay in English whichever lyrics language you pick, because the style fields on Suno and Flow Music are built around English.
Can I make an instrumental with no vocals?
Pick Instrumental under Vocal and you get a structure prompt with [Intro], [Main Theme], [Instrumental Break] and [Outro] instead of lyrics — handy for background music or short-form video.
Is anything I type saved?
No. Everything runs in this browser and is never sent to a server. Reloading the page clears your inputs and results, so copy anything you want to keep.
Is the background music copyrighted?
No. The audio, cover image and video on this page are generated on the spot by the browser's Web Audio and Canvas, so treat them as CC0 (public domain). Use them as background music in monetised YouTube, Shorts or Reels videos — no credit needed. YouTube's Content ID reacts to registered commercial recordings, so if someone else's video using this audio gets claimed, that is a separate matter.
What format are the WAV and the video saved in?
Audio is 44.1 kHz stereo 16-bit WAV, ready for an editor or YouTube. Video uses whatever codec the browser supports — WebM (VP9/Opus) or MP4. Vertical 9:16 is for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.
Why does making the video take so long?
It draws the frames in real time and records them, so it takes as long as the track. A 30-second track takes 30 seconds. Keep the tab in front while recording; frames drop if you switch away.

What the lyrics and prompts are — The sentence frames, word banks and layout on this page were written by INSIGHT BRIEF; no other lyric service or song was copied. Treat the output as a starting point: copyright, revenue and distribution of the finished track follow the terms of the AI music service you use.

Privacy — Theme, keywords and settings are processed only in your browser and are not stored on any server. Please don't put real names, phone numbers or addresses into the keyword field, and check the same before sharing a result.

Copyrighted material — Putting a particular singer's name, a real song title or a trademark into a prompt to make it “in that singer's voice” can raise publicity-right and trademark issues. Describing the style through genre and mood is safer.

Platform terms — Suno, Google Flow Music, Udio and similar services allow different levels of commercial use depending on the plan. Before monetising on YouTube, TikTok or a streaming service, read that platform's current terms. This tool has no partnership with any of them.

Synthesised audio, image and video — the background music on this page is synthesised by JavaScript with the Web Audio API, and the cover image and video are drawn with Canvas. No sample recordings or third-party artwork are used, so the results are free to use (CC0). Do not put a real person's name or a trademark in the title or lyrics.
