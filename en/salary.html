          Calculation formula

          Monthly conversion working hours and salary
          Add the weekly working hours to the weekly vacation time (if the weekly working hours are more than 15 hours, weekly working hours ÷ 5, maximum 8 hours) and multiply by 365/12/7. 40 hours a week is (40+8) × 4.3452 ≒ 209 hours, and multiply this by the hourly wage to get the monthly salary.
          Four major insurance premiums
          The national pension multiplies the standard monthly income (monthly compensation rounded off to less than 1,000 won, range from 410,000 to 6.59 million won) by 5%. Health insurance is 3.595% of the monthly salary (rounded down to 10 won or less), and long-term care is 13.90% of the health insurance premium (rounded down to 10 won or less). Employment insurance multiplies the monthly compensation amount by 0.9% (1.0% when reorganized), and the employer adds the job security and vocational skills development rate by size to this. Industrial accident insurance is fully borne by the employer at the rate of monthly compensation × (industry rate + 0.06%).
          Earned income tax
          From the National Tax Service’s Simplified Tax Amount Table on Earned Income (Appendix 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Income Tax Act) Monthly salary section excluding tax exemptionand Number of families eligible for deductionmeets. Local income tax is 10% of income tax. The simplified tax table is a value that reflects the earned income deduction, basic deduction, pension insurance premium deduction, and earned income tax credit in advance, so it is recalculated with the actual tax at the year-end tax settlement. It starts at the moment
          Severance pay
          If you continue to work for more than one year, upon retirement you will receive the average daily wage × 30 days × (number of days of employment ÷ 365). If you look at the monthly savings, it is about 1/12 of the monthly salary, so that amount was included in the company expenses (DC type retirement pension with a statutory saving obligation is more than 1/12 of the monthly salary).
          Annual paid leave
          Less than 1 year: 1 day per month of perfect attendance (up to 11 days). Attendance at least 80% of the time for more than one year: 15 days. Over 3 years: 1 day added for every 2 years of continuous employment beyond the first year, up to a total of 25 days. Unused allowance is the regular hourly wage × 8 hours × the number of unused days.
          National Pension
          Basic pension amount (per year) = 1.29 × (A + B) × (1 + 0.05 × n/12). 1.29 is the proportionality constant corresponding to an income replacement rate of 43%, and n is the number of months of subscription exceeding 20 years. If the subscription period is 10 to 20 years, the payment rate is multiplied by 50 to 100%, and if the subscription period is less than 10 years, a lump sum refund is received instead of a pension. The monthly amount is the annual amount divided by 12.
          Unemployment benefits
          Average daily wage = Total wages for the 3 months before changing jobs ÷ Number of days in that period (This calculator divides by 91.25 days). If the basic daily wage exceeds 113,500 won, the upper limit is applied, multiplied by 60%, and if it is lower than the lower limit (minimum wage × 80% × daily working hours), the lower limit is used. Multiply this by the number of salary days (120 to 270 days) to get the total amount.
