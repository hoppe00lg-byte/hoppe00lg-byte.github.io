How we checked

  • Dates and venues were checked on 24 November 2026 on each organiser's official site and in KOTRA's Global Exhibition Platform (GEP) exhibition records. Every card links to the page we checked; shows taken from GEP also carry a 'GEP page' link.
  • Shows without confirmed dates are not listed, and shows that GEP gives only as a month were left out. We do not copy estimated dates or last year's dates.
  • Where the organiser and GEP differ, we follow the organiser and note the difference. BIO International Convention 2027, for example, runs to June 10 in the organiser's announcement.
  • Where a show moved its dates or venue, the card notes it: NEPCON JAPAN 2027 moved from January to February, Automate 2027 from Chicago to Las Vegas, and SEMICON West 2027 from San Francisco to Phoenix.
  • Organisers can change dates, so check the official site once more before booking flights or hotels.

Source: KOTRA Global Exhibition Platform (GEP) — exhibition information and dates. For shows taken from GEP, the 'GEP page' link in the detail card opens that show's record.
