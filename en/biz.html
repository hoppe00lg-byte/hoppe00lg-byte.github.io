Calculation formula

  1. Customs value = goods + freight + insurance (already included in goods under CIF)
  2. Duty = customs value × duty rate
  3. Import VAT = (customs value + duty) × VAT rate — credited as input VAT
  4. Net unit cost = (total landed cost − import VAT) ÷ quantity
  5. Recommended price = net unit cost ÷ (1 − target margin)

The default exchange rate is the Seoul FX market close on 20 November 2026 (KRW/USD 1,330.8). Import VAT is money you recover as input VAT, so it is excluded from the net cost. Calculation formula

  1. Net cash = revenue − variable cost − fixed cost − interest − principal
  2. Equal payment = principal × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n) (r = annual rate ÷ 12, n = term)
  3. Equal principal = principal ÷ n + remaining principal × r
  4. Bullet = interest only, principal repaid in full at maturity

This assumes simple interest at a fixed annual rate. Real repayments vary with the bank's day-count, grace periods, early repayment fees and floating-rate resets. Revenue growth compounds at the same monthly rate, and variable costs are assumed to move in proportion to revenue. Taxes (VAT and income tax) are not included, so set aside the months when VAT is due.
