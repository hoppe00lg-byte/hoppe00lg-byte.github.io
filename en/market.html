01

Ironmaking Steel raw materials

Pig iron is made by reducing iron ore to coke, and iron scrap is used as the main raw material in electric furnaces.

  • Iron OreFe 62% standard product→Sinter/pellet→Therefore→Pig iron (hot iron)
  • Coking coalThe conclusion of the strong→Coke→Therefore
  • Iron Scrap→Electric Furnace EAF→Jo Kang

According to the World Steel Association average, making 1,000kg of crude steel requires 1,370kg of iron ore, 780kg of coking coal, 270kg of limestone, and 125kg of steel scrap for the blast furnace/converter route, and 710kg of iron scrap and 2.3GJ of electricity for the electric furnace route. One ton of pig iron requires 1.6 tons of iron ore and about 450 kg of coke.

02

Steelmaking & Semis Steelmaking/semi-finished products

The crude steel from the converter and electric furnace is continuously cast to make slabs, billets, and blooms. The shape of the semi-finished product determines the next rolled product.

SlabSlab
  • Converter BOF→Slab→Hot rolled steel sheet
BilletBillet
  • Converter/Electric Furnace→Billet→Rebar, section steel, wire rod
BloomBloom
  • Converter/Electric Furnace→Bloom→Rail/Large section steel With

Slabs are made of plates, billets are made of long sections and wire rods, and blooms are made of rails and large sections. Even if the same crude steel has a different casting cross section, different rolling lines are used.

03

Flat Products Plate products

Hot-rolled slabs are cold-rolled and plated to make thin plates for automobiles, home appliances, and construction.

  • Hot rolled HR→Cold rolled CR→Galvanized GI/GA→Automotive exterior sales, home appliances, architecture
  • Hot rolled HR→Pickling steel sheet PO→Automobiles and industrial machinery
  • Slab→Plate→Shipbuilding, bridges, pressure vessels
  • Cold rolled CR→Color steel plate→Building exterior and home appliances

The standard is KS D 3501 for hot rolling, KS D 3512 for cold rolling, and KS D 3506 for hot dip galvanizing. Pickling steel sheet (PO) is made by pickling hot rolled steel to remove scale, GI is made by soaking cold rolled steel in zinc, and GA is made by heat treating a zinc-iron alloy layer.

04

Long & Special Steel Long section steel, special steel

Reinforcing bars, section steel, and wire rods made from rolled billets support construction and industrial bases, and special steel and stainless steel with alloy elements added are used in automobiles, shipbuilding, and chemical facilities.

  • RebarSD400·SD500→Architecture/Civil Engineering
  • H-beam steel, rolled section steel→Architectural framework and plant
  • Seonjae→PC steel wire, wire rope, bolt
  • Special steel (alloy steel)→Automobile parts, tools, and bearings
  • STS 30418Cr·8Ni→Cooking utensils, construction, and food equipment
  • STS 316Mo 2~3%→Chemical facilities, marine, medical

The reinforcing bars are of the KS D 3504 (steel bar for reinforced concrete) standard, and SD400 has a yield strength of 400 MPa. The most widely used stainless steel is austenitic stainless steel (304·316), which contains over 10.5% of chromium plus nickel, and 316 contains 2~3% of molybdenum, making it resistant to salt and acid.

05

Copper Copper

Copper ore is smelted and electrolyzed to make electrolytic copper, which is then processed into wires, copper tubes, and copper foil.

CopperElectric building

  • Electric building→Copper foil→Secondary battery negative electrode current collector
  • Copper scrap→Regenerative Smelting→Electric copper/brass

The subject of LME trading is electrolytic copper of the Cu-CATH-1 standard, and the total impurities specified are less than 0.0065%, so the purity is indicated at around 99.99%. The USGS calculated that demand for copper and copper alloy products was 42% for construction, 23% for electricity and electronics, and 18% for transportation.

Based on the International Aluminum Association (IAI), 4 to 6 tons of bauxite produces 2 tons of alumina, and then 1 ton of primary aluminum. The electrolysis process requires approximately 14,000 kWh of power per ton.

Nickel is a key raw material for 300 series stainless steel and secondary battery precursors.

Raw Sugar Raw sugar

06

Coffee Coffee

The caffeine of Arabica coffee beans is 0.9-1.4%, and that of Robusta coffee beans is 1.7-4.0%, which is about twice that of Robusta. World production in 2023/24 was 57.4% Arabica and 42.6% Robusta (ICO).

For cocoa, international futures (ICE) and spot premiums move separately, and the domestic import cost must be considered together with raw material prices, exchange rates, tariffs, and distribution costs. There is no cocoa price in the market chart on this page.

08

Palm Oil Palm oil

Crude oil (CPO) is extracted from palm fruits, refined, and used as a raw material for cooking oil and oleochemicals.

  • Palm fruit FFB→CPO Cho Yu→Tablets (RBD)→Cooking oil, margarine, frying oil
  • CPO→Oil chemistryFatty acids and glycerin→Soap, detergent, cosmetics

The Malaysian palm oil extraction rate (OER) was 19.86% (2023), and the palm fruit yield was 15.79 tonnes per ha (MPOB). Fruit quality and maturity determine milking rates.

09

Natural Rubber Natural rubber

Latex is coagulated and dried to make sheets (RSS) or blocks (TSR), which are then used to make tires and industrial products.

  • Latex→Coagulation/Smoking→RSS3Smoked Sheet→Tires and rubber products

  • Latex→Concentrated latex→Gloves, medical supplies, bedding

TSR20 grades based on technical specifications such as pollution, ash, nitrogen, volatile matter, and plasticity retention index, while RSS3 grades smoke sheets visually. Thailand and Indonesia account for approximately 80% of world production (JPX).

Reference was made to USDA ERS·NASS (2025/26 yield), FAO food price index, Rural Development Administration, KAMIS, MPOB (2023), ICO, ICE contract specifications, and JPX natural rubber data. Yield and grade vary depending on the variety, production area, and process, so you must check the supplier's standards before signing a contract.

What is Unit Consumption?

How much does it cost to make 1 ton of product? Quantity of raw materialsis the value written in tones. 1 ton of HDPE contains 1.02 to 1.04 tons of ethylene, and 1 ton of PP contains 1.05 to 1.10 tons of propylene. The reason it is greater than 1 is because the reaction yield is not 100%, by-products and losses occur, and efficiency varies depending on the equipment and catalyst generation. 1.02~1.04is the difference in process method.

How to read a table — two directions

  • Resin Khan: The raw materials and amount used to make 1 ton of that item.
  • Product Khan: The amount of that item in 1 ton of other products.
  • Example: If you look for Acetone, you will find 2.27 to 2.35 Cumene (cumene method) in the raw material and 0.28 BPA in the product.
  • The sum may not be 1. Alternative routes (direct injection of BD / via BR) and intermediates (SAN) are listed together with the raw materials (SM), so adding them as is is double counting. For cost calculation, only one route is selected and used.

Cost calculation — raw material price × unit price

Raw material price (per ton) × won unit = theoretical raw material cost per ton. Utilities, labor costs, depreciation and margins are added here, and revenue from the sale of by-products (e.g. acetone from cumene-processed phenol) must be subtracted separately to get closer to the actual cost.

  • PP Propylene $1,088.25 × 1.05~1.10 = $1,143~1,197/ton
  • HDPE Ethylene $1,039.08 × 1.02~1.04 = $1,060~1,081/ton
  • Raw material prices are KCIA·CFR Northeast Asia average for the third quarter of 2026 (Indicator Nuri). Differences in fares, exchange rates, and levels are omitted.
