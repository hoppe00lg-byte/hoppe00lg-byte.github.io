Instead of pasting it as is, rewrite one line. If you promise services, discounts, or inventory that actually did not exist, you will become more dissatisfied later. If you reply back to an expression from a customer review, the same sentence will read much more authentically.

How to answer a complaint review

5 steps
STEP 01

Let’s check the facts first

Compare with records of when, what menu, and which staff. If you conclude that “that happened” before confirming, you will be in trouble later when the facts are different. When in doubt, start with “I’m just checking.”

STEP 02

Apologies come before excuses

If the first sentence is an excuse, guests won't read until the end. Start with “We apologize for the inconvenience,” followed by a short explanation of the situation.

STEP 03

The measures are specifically:

Rather than “I will try my best,” “I will rearrange the order of peak hours” gives more credibility. Just don't write that you've done something you haven't done yet.

STEP 04

Rewards are not used in public replies

If you write down the amount of refund, coupon, or compensation in a public reply, it will create a problem of fairness with other guests, and it will be recorded as an admission of responsibility. Inform them by saying, “We will contact you individually” and conclude with a message or phone call.

STEP 05

Avoid making the same mistake again

A reply is a message to one customer and a notice to be read by the next customer. If you add one line of improvement, the review becomes a source of trust.

What tone should I choose for 1-3 star reviews?
The default is ‘apple’. The safest order is apology → confirmation of facts → concrete action → information on personal channel. Use ‘humor’ only when a customer leaves a mild complaint, and do not use it when talking about hygiene, safety, or injuries.

It is better not to include amounts, coupons, or compensation in public replies. This can create equity issues with other guests and leave a record of admitting liability. Just inform them by saying, “We will contact you individually after confirmation,” and conclude with a message or phone call.
When is the best time to reply?
If possible, we recommend applying within 24 to 48 hours. Late replies to old reviews often do not reach customers. It's okay to cram it in once a day, at a set time.
Can I reply to the same review multiple times?
The rule is to apply once. Edit only when you need to correct a typo or factual error. If you edit your reply multiple times, it will appear to other customers as an unmanaged store.
What do I do about false/malicious reviews?
If the information is different from the facts, check the platform’s review reporting and objection procedures first. In a public reply, it is better to leave out your emotions and write only “confirmed results” briefly. If there is a threat or exposure of personal information, leave a capture and report it to the platform.

Privacy — Review text and store name are processed only within the browser and are not stored on our servers. Expressions that should not be used — Avoid expressions that assert unconfirmed facts, promise compensation amounts, medical or legal judgments, or disparaging expressions compared to other stores.
