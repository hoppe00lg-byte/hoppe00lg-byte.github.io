Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · INSIGHT BRIEF (insightbriefnews.com)

INSIGHT BRIEF is an independent research webzine. We process only the information we need and keep it safe. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

2. Why we use it

  • To serve and display content, including articles and free tools
  • To measure traffic, improve quality, and respond to errors or abuse
  • To display advertising through third-party advertising partners
  • To verify and reply to inquiries and news tips

3. Cookies and third-party services

We use cookies for convenience, statistics and advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work if you do.

  • Google Analytics (measurement ID G-GXYJ52M3GC) — traffic statistics such as visits, time on page and referrers
  • Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-7853253075143438) — ad delivery. Google and third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ad Settings and from third-party vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Hosting — the site is served through GitHub Pages, whose own policies apply to access handling.
  • External links — articles and tools may link to government, public or company sites and to affiliate partners. Information handling there follows that site's policy.

4. Retention

  • Inquiries: deleted once the reply and any follow-up are complete; records may be kept where needed to handle a correction or right of reply.
  • Automatically collected data: deleted once the statistical purpose is served, subject to the analytics retention settings and the host's log policy.
  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information for advertising or marketing purposes. The statistics, advertising and hosting services above process information under their own policies, and we comply with lawful requests from authorities where the law requires it.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, or to restrict its processing. Write to the contact below; we will verify your identity and act without undue delay.

7. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that we have, we delete it promptly.

8. Security

The site is served entirely over HTTPS; inquiry information is accessible only to the person handling it, and access is kept to a minimum.

9. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions or requests, use the contact page or write to contact@insightbriefnews.com.
